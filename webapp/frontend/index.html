in one run

UUpload a set of bacterial genome assemblies and get a complete taxonomic profile in return: genome quality with CheckM2, pairwise identity across ANI, dDDH, AAI and POCP, and a phylogenomic tree built with FastANI, BLASTN, EzAAI, DIAMOND and EasyCGTree. Results come as plates you can read, not just raw numbers.

Every step is optional: pick exactly the analyses you need.
