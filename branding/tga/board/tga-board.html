What we heard

The brief

A bioinformatics pipeline that decides, from whole genomes, whether bacterial strains belong to the same species or genus (ANI, dDDH, AAI, POCP, core-gene phylogenomics). Its audience is microbiologists and taxonomists; its interface is set like a natural-history atlas plate.

rigorousscholarlypreciseclassificatory

Avoid the double helix and dotted-node trees; read as TGA; belong to the atlas interface (paper, ink, Prussian blue); survive as a 16 px favicon.

Concept A

Dendrogram

The letters T, G and A are the leaves of a dendrogram — the same figure the pipeline draws.

  • The mark is the tool's own output: G and A are sisters, T joins them higher up
  • Bodoni Moda letters and Prussian-blue branches match the atlas interface
  • A simplified tree-and-leaves cut keeps it legible as a 16 px favicon
